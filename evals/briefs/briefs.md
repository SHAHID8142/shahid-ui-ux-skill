skill (plain agent output = the comparison floor). Provenance of each
baseline is recorded in its bundle: builder-agent (GLM via ZCode),
2026-11-14, unassisted generation from these briefs only.

Landing section for "Meridian", a fictional note-taking app for solo

primary CTA, three feature explanations, one testimonial, footer. Audience:
academics; tone: plain, evidence-minded, no hype.

content-queue table (4 rows with status), a settings form. Dense, functional,
Operate mode (D20).
