## What each playbook decides

**`onboarding`** — the flow's job is one completed real task, not a tour. Step one
does work; anything changeable later is skippable through a plain link that says
what happens; progress is stored per step so a person who leaves returns to the
same place with a usable account. A failed step keeps everything entered and stays
put. The ending lands in the workspace with the created thing visible — a
celebration screen that returns to nothing is not an ending.

**`article-layout`** — endurance over first impression. A 65–75 character measure at
every width, a contents list that marks the current section with
`aria-current="location"` and never scroll-jacks, code and tables that scroll inside
their own container instead of widening the page, stable focusable heading anchors
(renaming a heading must not silently break a published link), and print that drops
chrome and expands the contents.

**`gallery-case-study`** — the work is the interface. The first viewport is the
artifact; tiles reserve their space so arriving images never move the grid under a
click; the image dialog traps focus, closes on Escape and returns focus to the tile;
and an outcome is attributed to a client and period or labelled *illustrative*,
because art direction is not evidence.
