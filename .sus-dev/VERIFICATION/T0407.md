## The six table states, and why they are not five

Sort, filter and pagination are *state in the URL*, so a shared link and a refresh
keep the operator's place. The two empties are separate on purpose: "no records yet"
offers *create*, "none matching your filters" offers *clear the filters*. Collapsing
them into "No results" produces the classic dead end where an operator with a stale
filter believes the database is empty.

Loading does not replace the rows. The previous page stays visible with the toolbar
stating that results are loading, because an operator who loses their visual position
on every keystroke cannot compare anything. Errors keep the rows too, with an inline
retry — clearing a table is never an error treatment.

## Record forms

One save model per form, stated on the form (submit or autosave, never both
silently); conflicts state what changed and offer a choice rather than overwriting a
colleague's edit; deletion is recoverable or requires typed confirmation naming the
record; a failed save keeps every entered value; submit is guarded in the handler as
well as the UI; at narrow widths the primary save never overlaps a field.
