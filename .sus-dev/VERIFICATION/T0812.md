## The two complex matrices the task named

**`file-upload`** — N parallel async objects, each with its own state. Per file: queued,
uploading (progress), done, failed with reason, cancelled with a statement of what
happened to the partial. Retry **resumes the failed file only** and never re-sends
the nine that already succeeded. Cancel stops the transfer, removes the partial
where the server supports it, and says so; an orphan nobody can see is the failure
mode being designed out. Constraints (type, size, count) are stated before the drop
and enforced again on the server, because the client check is a courtesy. Drag is an
enhancement: the dropzone is a real button and paste works where meaningful.

**`date-picker`** — typing is always possible and always faster: the input accepts the
locale's format and an unpadded ISO form, displays the locale format, and never
silently reinterprets a day/month swap. The calendar is a real grid with roving
tabindex, arrows/PageUp/PageDown/Home/End/Enter/Escape, and `aria-selected` days
whose accessible name is the full date. Refusals carry reasons (before project start,
not a business day) both on disabled days and on typed values. Calendar dates stay
dates and instants are stored in UTC with the zone shown — the off-by-one-day bug is
a modelling bug, not a formatting one.

## The other four

`tooltip` reachable on keyboard focus as well as hover, `aria-describedby` not
`aria-labelledby`, Escape dismisses without moving focus, and interactive content is a
popover instead. `progress` only determinate when the total is real, announced at most
about once a second, 100 % is an outcome and not a claim of success. `skeleton` only
when the arriving shape is known, always the final geometry, becomes a stated wait
after about ten seconds, and hands over explicitly on failure. `command-palette`
permission-filters before it ranks, keeps focus in the input with
`aria-activedescendant`, discards stale responses by sequence id, and never makes a
destructive command the default active option.

## Honest limitations

- These are contracts, not components: no rendered file-upload or date picker exists
  yet. Their reference implementations come with T0906's dogfood sites.
- "Extra states" was satisfied inside the existing eight-state vocabulary
  (`active` carries cancel/drag-over, `loading` carries progress, `error` carries
  retry). No new schema vocabulary was invented to look thorough.
- Locale behaviour is specified, not tested: there is no fixture that types
  `03/04/2026` in two locales and asserts both parses.
- Provenance unchanged: three claim ids, so our own decisions stay labelled
  `convention`.
