## What the gap actually was

The launch set was already covered by component count (15 promised; 16 authored
across T0405/T0405c/T0407). The real gap was the **standard**, not the count: my
playbooks satisfied parts 1–6 and skimmed 7–12 — accessibility, responsive
behaviour, alternatives, graded references and failure cases were implied by rules
rather than recorded. A playbook can pass that way and still tell an agent nothing
about keyboard behaviour or what a failure looks like, which is exactly the kind of
silent gap "same standard" is supposed to rule out.

## The fix, in two layers

1. **Contract** (`packages/schemas/src/playbook.ts`): `sections` is now a required
   field covering PRD §6 parts 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12; parts 5 and 6 are
   satisfied by the existing `stateMachine` + `variantMatrix`. References must
   carry a `YYYY-MM-DD` date and an evidence grade — an undated citation is not a
   reference.
2. **Gate** (`pnpm parts`, wired into `verify`): counts the parts per playbook and
   names the missing PRD numbers, so omission reads as "missing part 7
   (accessibility)" rather than "invalid schema at sections.a11y.0".

## Honest grading of the references

Every playbook cites three references, and most are graded `convention` because
that is what they are — our own decisions. Only real specifications are graded
`normative-standard` (WCAG 2.2 success criteria, WAI-ARIA APG, HTML Living
Standard, CSS `prefers-reduced-motion`). I did **not** invent research citations to
look better: claiming a research grade for a convention would be the exact
provenance dishonesty G10 and the provenance catalog exist to prevent. A test
asserts that conventions are labelled as such.

## Honest limitations

- Parts 9 (copy/SEO) is an array that may be empty, because forbidding empty would
  force filler in components where copy is not the decision. Emptiness is a
  recorded decision, not an omission — but it is also the part easiest to skip, and
  the gate cannot tell "not applicable" from "not done".
- Acceptance (part 12) is written as testable sentences but is not yet *executed*;
  binding them to runnable checks is T0708/T0906 work.
- References remain thin for the same reason as batch 1: the provenance catalog has
  three claim ids, so external evidence cannot honestly be cited yet.
