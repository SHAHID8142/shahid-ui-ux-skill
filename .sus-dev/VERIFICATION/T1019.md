**Task:** issue #13 names the audit pipeline's checks as "responsive/zoom/keyboard/semantic/a11y".
Responsive and zoom are measured (G7/G8), semantics by `seo-structure`, accessibility by axe (G4) —
and **keyboard had no instrument at all**. The dogfood runner tests the *first* focus ring, which is
not a traversal: a page can pass that and then drop focus on the second Tab.

## The measurement bug, kept in the record

The first live run against `fixtures/portfolio/dist` reported **"Tab order leaves the document
2 time(s)"** and **"Focus cycles through a.wordmark"**. Both were my scanner's fault, not the
fixture's: 30 Tab presses over 9 controls wrap around the document, and at the wrap Chromium parks
focus on `body` for a moment — so a naive counter sees "detours" and "repeats". Reporting that would
have been exactly the invented defect this project keeps refusing to ship.

The corrected definition separates two things a body stop can mean:

- **controls still unreached afterwards** → focus vanished mid-page → a real detour (P1);
- **every control already reached** → the traversal ended and the browser moved to its own chrome
  → normal, not a finding. The traversal is truncated there, and cycles are only looked for before
  the truncation, over a repeated suffix of period 1–3 rather than a bare repeat count.

`keyboard.test.ts` now pins that distinction in both directions: the vanishing fixture still reports
a detour, and a clean three-control page pressed 10 times reports `endReached: true`, `detours: 0`,
`cycle: null`.

## Measured

```
./packages/eval-core/dist/cli.js audit --path ./fixtures/portfolio/dist --out /tmp/sus-audit-kb2 --mode deep
keyboard-flow  pass   30 Tab stop(s) recorded across 1 route(s)
```

No keyboard findings on our own fixture, with 30 stops actually driven — a measurement, not a
default. The same run still reports what the fixture genuinely has (`axe-a11y` P0
`scrollable-region-focusable`, a 400% zoom overflow, tap targets at 390 px), which is how a check
should look: it passes one thing and fails another in the same report.

## What this does not claim

- **Escape, arrow keys and roving tabindex** are not pressed. A modal's intentional trap therefore
  shows up as a P2 cycle, and the finding says that is what it is: an observation.
- **Screen-reader announcement** stays needs-review. Nothing here reads the accessibility tree.
- **Indicator adequacy** is not judged: an outline is recorded as present, not as sufficient
  contrast or size.
- **Detours past 30 presses** cannot be seen. The bound is deliberate, and the detail line records
  how many stops were driven so a truncated traversal cannot read as a complete one.
